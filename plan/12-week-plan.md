# 12-Week Roadmap

Think of it like software releases: each phase builds on the last.

## Phase 1 – Foundation (weeks 1–4) · "v0.1"
**Goal:** build consistency, not intensity.

| Area | Habit |
|------|-------|
| Diet | Water before meals · vegetable at lunch and dinner · no sugary drinks |
| Exercise | Walk 20–30 min daily · 2 short full-body sessions/week |
| Mind | Fixed bedtime · 10 min reading or learning · no phone first 30 min of the day |

✅ Done when: you hit 80% of your habits for 2 weeks in a row.

## Phase 2 – Build (weeks 5–8) · "v0.5"
**Goal:** add structure and progression.

| Area | Habit |
|------|-------|
| Diet | Protein in every meal · weekly meal prep · plan meals for the week |
| Exercise | 3 strength sessions/week with progression · 8,000+ steps/day |
| Mind | Daily 25-min deep-work block · 5 min breathing/meditation · journal |

✅ Done when: workouts are getting heavier/harder and energy is better.

## Phase 3 – Level up (weeks 9–12) · "v1.0"
**Goal:** performance and making it your lifestyle.

| Area | Habit |
|------|-------|
| Diet | 80/20 rule (80% whole foods) · eat out smart · tune portions to results |
| Exercise | 3–4 sessions/week · add a cardio goal (5 km, a hike, a class) |
| Mind | Learn one skill with a 30-day plan · weekly digital-detox block |

✅ Done when: compare with your baseline and write `plan/results.md`.

## After week 12
Repeat the cycle with new goals ("v2.0"). Ask Claude with `prompts/00-start-here.md` again, adding your results.
