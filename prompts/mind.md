# Sharp mind prompts 🧠

### 1. Focus routine
> Design a daily focus routine for me. I get distracted by [phone, social media…]. Include a deep-work method (e.g. Pomodoro 25/5), phone rules, and how to start the day.

### 2. Learn a skill fast
> I want to learn [skill: English, coding, Git, guitar…]. I have [minutes] per day. Create a 30-day learning plan using active recall and spaced repetition, with a mini-test every week.

### 3. Quiz me
> Quiz me with 10 questions about [topic I studied today]. Ask one at a time, wait for my answer, then explain.

### 4. Reading habit
> Recommend 5 books about [habits / focus / psychology / health] for a beginner, and a plan to read 10 pages a day.

### 5. Sleep reset
> I sleep [hours] and go to bed at [time]. Build me a sleep routine to get 7–9 hours: evening routine, caffeine cutoff, screens, room setup.

### 6. Stress & anxiety tools
> Teach me 3 quick techniques (under 5 minutes) to calm down when I'm stressed, like box breathing, and when to use each.

### 7. Daily journal
> Ask me 5 short journaling questions for the evening: gratitude, what went well, what I learned, what to improve, tomorrow's top 3 tasks.

### 8. Brain games
> Give me a daily 10-minute brain workout: memory, mental math, and a logic puzzle. Increase difficulty each day.

**Pillars of a sharp mind**
- Sleep 7–9 h (the #1 brain booster)
- Exercise (boosts memory and mood)
- Learn something new daily (15–30 min)
- Less phone, more deep focus
- 5–10 min of meditation or breathing
