# Exercise prompts 🏃

### 1. Beginner full-body program
> Create a 3-days-per-week full-body program for a beginner at [home/gym] with [equipment]. 30–45 min per session. Include warm-up, exercises, sets, reps, rest, and how to progress each week. Explain proper form in simple words.

### 2. No-equipment 20-minute workout
> Give me a 20-minute no-equipment workout I can do in my room, with easier and harder options for each exercise.

### 3. Progress my program
> Last week I did: [exercises, sets, reps, weights, how hard it felt 1–10]. How should I progress this week?

### 4. Cardio plan
> Build me a plan to go from [walking 20 min] to [running 5 km / 10,000 steps a day] in 8 weeks.

### 5. Mobility & back pain
> I sit [hours] a day. Give me a 10-minute daily mobility routine for hips, back, and shoulders.

### 6. Motivation when I don't want to train
> I don't feel like working out today. Give me a 5-minute minimum workout and one reason to do it anyway.

**Weekly target (general guidelines)**
- 150+ min moderate activity (brisk walking counts)
- 2–3 strength sessions
- 7,000–10,000 steps a day
- Daily stretching/mobility 5–10 min
