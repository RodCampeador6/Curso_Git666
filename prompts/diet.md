# Diet prompts 🥗

Use one at a time. Replace the `[brackets]`.

### 1. Weekly meal plan
> Create a 7-day meal plan for a [goal: fat loss / muscle / maintenance] with about [calories or "no counting"] using the plate method (½ vegetables, ¼ protein, ¼ carbs). Budget: [ ]. I cook [skill level] and have [minutes] per meal. Include a grocery list grouped by store section.

### 2. Meal prep in 2 hours
> I want to meal prep on Sunday in 2 hours for 5 lunches and 5 dinners. Give me recipes, the order to cook them, and how to store them.

### 3. Healthier version of my favorite food
> I love [pizza / tacos / burgers…]. Give me 3 healthier versions that still taste great, with protein and calories estimates.

### 4. Fix my current day
> Here is what I ate today: [list]. What did I do well, and what 2 small changes would make the biggest difference?

### 5. Snacks & cravings
> Give me 15 healthy snacks under 200 kcal with good protein, and a strategy for night cravings.

### 6. Eating out
> I'm going to [restaurant type]. What should I order to stay on track?

### 7. Protein check
> My weight is [ ] and my goal is [ ]. How much protein do I need per day and how can I hit it with normal foods across 3–4 meals?

**Easy habits to start with**
- Water glass before every meal
- Protein + vegetable in every meal
- Swap soda/juice for water, sparkling water or tea
- Stop eating 2–3 h before bed
