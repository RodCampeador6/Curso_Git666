# Weekly review prompt 🔁

Every Sunday, paste your weekly log after this prompt:

---

You are my health coach. Here is my week:

[paste tracking/week-XX.md here]

Please:
1. Tell me what went well (be specific).
2. Find the 1–2 biggest things holding me back.
3. Suggest ONE new small habit or adjustment for next week (diet, exercise, or mind).
4. Adjust my workouts if needed (progression or deload).
5. Give me a score from 1–10 for consistency and one sentence of motivation.

Keep it short and practical.
